CHROME EXTENSION PRIVACY POLICY

Private post-game coaching, by design.

Last updated July 31, 2026. The extension's single purpose is to analyze completed public Chess.com games locally and recommend opening lessons.

Information handled

  • The Chess.com username you enter and save.
  • Extension preferences.
  • Public completed-game PGNs and metadata retrieved from Chess.com.
  • Local Stockfish review results.
  • A visible game-over signal. The extension does not read chat, passwords, authentication cookies, or private messages.

Use and storage

Your username is sent over HTTPS only to api.chess.com to retrieve public data. The username, settings, completed game, and analysis are stored in Chrome's local extension storage. Stockfish runs locally.

Repertoire /64 does not receive this information on a developer server, sell it, use it for advertising, or make it available for human review.

Permissions

  • Storage saves local settings and reviews.
  • Alarms waits for the completed PGN to become available.
  • Notifications tells you a review is ready.
  • Chess.com page access detects only the visible completed-game state.
  • api.chess.com access retrieves public player and completed-game data.

Retention and control

Information remains locally until you overwrite it, clear extension data, or uninstall the extension. You can change the username or disable automatic review at any time.

Limited Use

Information is used only for the disclosed post-game coaching features. Repertoire /64 complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact and changes

Material data-practice changes will be disclosed before they begin. Support and privacy questions can be submitted through the support contact on the Chrome Web Store listing.
